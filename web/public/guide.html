    How to give VAT software access to HMRC

    1. Authorise with HMRC

      Choose Retrieve obligations, then sign in to HMRC and give permission once; later requests reuse that permission.

    2. The answer

      Read the obligations table that HMRC returns straight away, with no second sign-in.

    How to view your VAT obligations under MTD

    These pictures come from HMRC's test service, which uses periods from 2017. Your screen shows your own periods.

    1. See your VAT periods

      Read what this page does: it lists the VAT periods HMRC expects a return for.

    2. Already signed in

      Check that the header shows you signed in; if it does not, choose Sign in first.

    3. Open VAT Obligations

      Choose VAT Obligations (HMRC) on the Activities page.

    4. Enter your VAT number

      Enter your VAT registration number.

    5. Choose the start date

      Enter a From date and a To date to search between; both are optional.

    6. Ask HMRC for access

      Choose Retrieve obligations; the first request sends you to HMRC to give permission.

    7. The answer

      Read each period's due date and status in the obligations table.

    How to submit a VAT return under MTD

    These pictures come from HMRC's test service, which uses periods from 2017. Your screen shows your own periods.

    1. Ask HMRC

      Enter your VAT registration number and the dates to search between, then choose Retrieve obligations.

    2. The periods on file

      Read the periods HMRC holds for you: a Fulfilled period already has its return, and an Open period is waiting for one.

    3. Start the return

      Choose Submit return beside the open period.

    4. Box 1, VAT on sales

      Enter box 1, the VAT due in the period on sales and other outputs.

    5. Box 2, usually zero

      Enter box 2, the VAT due on acquisitions of goods made in Northern Ireland from EU member states. Enter 0 if you have none.

    6. Box 3 adds it up

      Check box 3, the total VAT due. Submit adds boxes 1 and 2 for you.

    7. Box 4, VAT reclaimed

      Enter box 4, the VAT you reclaim in the period on purchases and other inputs.

    8. Box 5, the net total

      Check box 5, the net VAT to pay to HMRC or reclaim. Submit works it out from boxes 3 and 4.

    9. Box 6, total sales

      Enter box 6, the total value of your sales and other outputs, excluding VAT.

    10. Box 7, total purchases

      Enter box 7, the total value of your purchases and other inputs, excluding VAT.

    11. Boxes 8 and 9, EU trade

      Enter boxes 8 and 9, the value of goods supplied to and acquired from EU member states, excluding VAT. Enter 0 if you have none.

    12. Confirm the declaration

      Tick the declaration that the information is true and complete.

    13. Authorise again

      Choose Submit VAT return, then sign in to HMRC and give permission again, because sending a return needs a wider grant than reading obligations.

    14. Submitted

      Read the receipt that HMRC returns, with its Form Bundle Number, and keep it as your record.

    15. Read back what you filed

      Choose Activities to go back to the list of what you can do.

    16. Open View VAT Return

      Choose View VAT Return (HMRC).

    17. Choose the period

      Enter the start and end dates of the period you filed.

    18. Ask HMRC for the record

      Choose View return to ask HMRC for the return on file.

    19. HMRC sends the record

      Wait for HMRC to send back the return it holds.

    20. Your record, as submitted

      Check the totals and the declaration in your copy against what you submitted.

    How to file a confirmation statementCS01

    1. Which company?

      Enter your company number and choose Look up company. Companies House confirms when the next statement is due.

    2. The register

      Check the current directors and the persons with significant control against what you know of the company.

    3. Use your own presenter

      Tick File under my own Companies House presenter to use your own presenter. Companies House then bills its fee to you.

    4. Enter the presenter id

      Enter your Presenter id.

    5. Enter the presenter code

      Enter your Presenter authentication code.

    6. Enter the auth code

      Enter the Company authentication code, the 6 to 8 character code that Companies House issued for the company.

    7. Read the register data

      Choose Read register data to fill in the statement from the register.

    8. Review and change

      Check each section, starting with the confirmation statement date, and change anything that is out of date.

    9. Confirm the directors

      Enter each director's other forenames and Companies House personal code, then tick the lawful purpose statement and the statement of confirmation.

    10. Preview

      Choose Preview confirmation statement and read it as it will be filed. Nothing is sent to Companies House yet.

    11. The filing

      Choose Submit to Companies House, wait for the outcome, then read your copy with its submission number, status, date and statement.

    How to file micro-entity accounts

    1. Find the company

      Choose File Micro-entity Accounts (Companies House) on the activities page.

    2. Enter the company number

      Enter your company number.

    3. Look up the company

      Choose Look up company.

    4. Confirm the company name

      Check that the company name Companies House shows is yours.

    5. Set the accounting period

      Enter the first and last day of the accounting period as Period start and Period end.

    6. This year, then last year

      Enter each balance sheet line for this year and then for last year: fixed assets, current assets, creditors due within one year, called up share capital, profit and loss account, and capital and reserves.

    7. Net assets must balance

      Check that the net assets shown equal your capital and reserves.

    8. Name the approving director

      Enter the name of the director who approves the balance sheet and the date of approval.

    9. Accept the statements

      Tick the four statements under Statements to accept each one.

    10. Preview

      Choose Preview accounts, then read the accounts as they will be filed. Nothing is sent to Companies House yet.

    11. File it

      Enter the company's authentication code and check the cost, one token, then choose Submit to Companies House.

    12. Companies House checks it

      Wait while Companies House checks the filing.

    13. Companies House has the filing

      Read the status that Companies House reports for the filing.

    14. Open your copy

      Choose See your copy of this filing.

    15. The filed accounts

      Read your copy: the filed accounts, the submission number and the status.

    16. Kept on your receipts

      Find your copy of the filing on the Receipts page whenever you need it.
