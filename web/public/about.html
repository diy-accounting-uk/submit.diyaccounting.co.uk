    What it does

    A VAT return form with nine boxes

    Enter your figures in the familiar 9-box format. Boxes 3 and 5 calculate automatically. Clear labels and hints help you enter the right values.

    Confirmation from HMRC

    Get confirmation when HMRC accepts your return. Your receipt includes the Form Bundle Number as official proof of submission.

    Your obligations

    See which VAT periods need returns and their due dates, with open and fulfilled obligations marked in words as well as colour.

    Returns you submitted

    Retrieve any previously submitted return directly from HMRC. Check that your figures were recorded correctly at any time.

    Why choose DIY Accounting Submit?

    Free guest tier
    Request a day pass for free access with 3 tokens, valid for 24 hours. Each VAT submission uses 1 token. No pass needed: visit the Bundles page and click Request. Up to 100 guests can hold this bundle at once; if it is full, try again the next day. Invited guests get a pass code for longer access instead.
    HMRC recognised
    Compliant with Making Tax Digital for VAT. Authorised to submit directly to HMRC.
    Bridging software
    Keep using your spreadsheet or accounting software. Enter your final figures here to submit.
    Accessible design
    Built to WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Works with screen readers and keyboard navigation.
