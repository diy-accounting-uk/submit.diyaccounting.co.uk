    What Submit files

    • VAT returns to HMRC, with the 9-box form, your obligations, the returns you submitted and the receipt HMRC gives back.
    • Company accounts and confirmation statements to Companies House.

    Submit in Welsh

    Submit gives these services in Welsh. Choose Cymraeg at the top of any page, and Submit remembers the choice.

    • The site's pages: the home page, getting started, Bundles, redeeming a pass, tools, the MTD calendar, rates and allowances, spreadsheets and signing in.
    • VAT with HMRC: submitting a VAT return, VAT obligations, liabilities, payments, penalties, viewing a VAT return and your receipts.
    • Companies House: company search, the watch list, your filings, micro-entity accounts, small company accounts, the confirmation statement, the registered office and the registered email address.
    • Site information: help and FAQs, the privacy policy, the terms of use and the accessibility statement.
