DIY Accounting Submit

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Quarterly updates for the self-employed and landlords

Status: sandbox. This runs against HMRC's sandbox. Nothing you send reaches your real HMRC record. Production waits on HMRC's recognition of our software after its 2027 update.

Synthetic mode - calls go to HMRC's sandbox API

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax replaces the annual Self Assessment return with a running record: self-employed people and landlords send HMRC a summary of income and expenses every quarter instead of once a year. This preview runs against HMRC's sandbox in every environment except production.

Pick a business

The steps below link to the file family the picked business uses - self-employment, UK property or foreign property - because HMRC's endpoints, field names and even the quarterly model differ between them.

This is two letters, six numbers and a final letter, for example QQ 12 34 56 C. You can find it on your National Insurance card, payslip or P60.

This service does not yet handle this type of business. Finish that part of your return with other MTD-compatible software or HMRC's online service - see HMRC's software finder.

7. Annual submission

Load the year's annual submission for the picked business, enter adjustments and allowances, and save them to HMRC.

Go to annual submission

8. Year-end adjustments

Request the year-end summary, read the net profit HMRC computed, and change a figure if it is wrong.

Go to year-end adjustments

9. Losses and claims

Applies if the picked business made a loss this year, or carries one from an earlier year. Every business's loss carries forward the same way; a sole trade can also carry sideways or back.

Go to losses and claims

10. Tax liability adjustments

Applies only if a carry-back claim was made in Losses and Claims. This is the second half of that claim - it tells HMRC what the carry-back saves.

Go to tax liability adjustments

11. Tax calculation

Request a calculation and see what the tax year owes, with one row per business.

Go to tax calculation

12. Final declaration
