Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Quarterly updates for the self-employed and landlords

Activities Receipts Bundles Spreadsheets
⚠️ SYNTHETIC MODE - calls go to HMRC's sandbox API

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax replaces the annual Self Assessment return with a running record: self-employed people and landlords send HMRC a summary of income and expenses every quarter instead of once a year. This preview runs against HMRC's sandbox in every environment except production.

Pick a Business

The steps below link to the file family the picked business uses - self-employment or property - because HMRC's endpoints, field names and even the quarterly model differ between them.

1. Business Details

Look up the self-employment and property businesses HMRC holds against your National Insurance number.

Go to Business Details

2. Obligations

Check which quarterly updates are open, due, or already sent, grouped by business.

Go to Obligations

3. File a Quarterly Update

Send a period summary of income and expenses to HMRC for the picked business.

Go to File a Quarterly Update

4. Period Summaries

List the quarterly updates already filed for the picked business.

Go to Period Summaries

5. View a Period Summary

Retrieve one filed quarterly update by tax year and period ID.

Go to View a Period Summary

6. Amend a Period Summary

Correct the income and expenses on a quarterly update already filed with HMRC.

Go to Amend a Period Summary
Year end

7. Annual Submission

Load the year's annual submission for the picked business, enter adjustments and allowances, and save them to HMRC.

Go to Annual Submission

8. Year-End Adjustments

Trigger the year-end summary, read the net profit HMRC computed, and change a figure if it is wrong.

Go to Adjustments

9. Losses and Claims

Applies if the picked business made a loss this year, or carries one from an earlier year. Every business's loss carries forward the same way; a sole trade can also carry sideways or back.

Go to Losses and Claims

10. Tax Liability Adjustments

Applies only if a carry-back claim was made in Losses and Claims. This is the second half of that claim - it tells HMRC what the carry-back saves.

Go to Tax Liability Adjustments

11. Tax Calculation

Trigger a calculation and see what the tax year owes, with one row per business.

Go to Tax Calculation

12. Final Declaration

Read the calculation again, confirm it is complete and correct for every business, and file the return.

Go to Final Declaration
