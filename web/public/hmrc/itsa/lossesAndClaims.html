Losses brought forward

The amount of earlier losses applied in this tax year. Leave at 0 if none.

Carry forward

The ordinary first-year-of-trading case, and any bad year. Also how a landlord's rental loss carries.

Carry sideways

Set this year's trading loss against this year's other income. Real for a sole trader who also has a job.

Carry back

Carries a loss back to an earlier year. Not available for a property business.

Only meaningful when both a carry-sideways and a carry-back claim are made in the same year.
