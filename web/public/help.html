    Submission

    1. Sign in with your Google or Microsoft account.
    2. Open Check VAT obligations to see which periods need a return.
    3. Click Submit VAT Return on the home page.
    4. Enter your figures in Boxes 1 to 9. Boxes 3 and 5 calculate for you.
    5. Tick the declaration that the information is true and complete.
    6. Click Submit. HMRC asks you to authorise the submission.
    7. Save your receipt. It shows the HMRC Form Bundle Number.

    An obligation is a period for which HMRC expects a VAT return. Each one shows three things.

    • VAT period is the range of dates the return covers, for example 1 January to 31 March 2026.
    • Due date is the day the return must reach HMRC.
    • Status is Open until you submit the return, and Fulfilled after.

    You can submit a return only for an Open obligation. Use Check VAT obligations to see yours.

    You cannot amend a submitted VAT return in Submit. How you correct it depends on the size of the error.

    • A small error goes on your next VAT return. It is small when its net value is £10,000 or less, or up to £50,000 if it is no more than 1% of your Box 6 figure for the return period in which you find it. It must be inside the 4-year time limit and not deliberate.
    • Any other error goes to HMRC on form VAT652, or in a letter.

    See HMRC guidance on correcting VAT errors. Keep your Submit receipt as a record of what you first submitted.

    If no obligations appear:

    1. Widen the date range. Obligations show only for periods in which HMRC expects a return.
    2. Check the status filter. It shows only Open obligations until you choose "All statuses".
    3. Wait after a new registration. HMRC can take 24 to 48 hours to create your first obligation after you register for Making Tax Digital.
    4. Check that the 9-digit VAT registration number is correct.

    These causes explain most mismatches:

    1. Rounding can differ. HMRC accepts figures to 2 decimal places for Boxes 1 to 5. Check that your spreadsheet rounds the same way.
    2. Boxes 3 and 5 calculate for you. Box 3 is Box 1 plus Box 2. Box 5 is the difference between Box 3 and Box 4, shown as a positive number. Do not calculate them yourself.
    3. Boxes 1 to 5 hold VAT amounts. Boxes 6 to 9 hold net values that exclude VAT.
    4. Boxes 6 to 9 are whole pounds with no pence.
    5. The period dates must match the obligation exactly. Check that your spreadsheet covers the same dates.

    A negative Box 5 means HMRC owes you a refund. It happens when Box 4 (VAT reclaimed) is more than Box 3 (VAT due). Submit shows Box 5 as a positive amount, the difference between Box 3 and Box 4, and HMRC knows from the figures whether it is a payment or a refund.

    HMRC then processes the refund by its normal procedure. Refunds usually reach your bank account within 10 working days. Your HMRC online account shows the status.

    Submit sends your VAT return. It does not take payment. If you owe VAT, pay HMRC separately:

    1. Open HMRC's page for paying VAT.
    2. Choose a payment method: Direct Debit, bank transfer (use your VAT registration number as the reference), or debit or credit card.

    The payment deadline is usually the same as the return deadline, and your obligations show it. A late payment and a late submission carry different penalties.

    Period codes such as 26AF and 26AG are HMRC's own references for VAT periods. You do not need to read them.

    The first two digits are the year (26 is 2026). The letters show the quarter or month, and they change with the scheme. Choose a period by its dates and its due date, and ignore the code.

    VAT Basics

    Box Description
    1 VAT due on sales and other outputs
    2 VAT due on acquisitions from EU (usually £0 post-Brexit)
    3 Total VAT due (Box 1 plus Box 2), calculated automatically
    4 VAT reclaimed on purchases and other inputs
    5 Net VAT to pay or reclaim (Box 3 minus Box 4), calculated automatically
    6 Total value of sales excluding VAT (whole pounds)
    7 Total value of purchases excluding VAT (whole pounds)
    8 Total value of supplies to EU excluding VAT (usually £0 post-Brexit)
    9 Total value of acquisitions from EU excluding VAT (usually £0 post-Brexit)

    You enter Boxes 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9. Boxes 3 and 5 are calculated for you.

    Your VAT registration number is 9 digits, with no spaces, letters or GB prefix. It is on your VAT registration certificate from HMRC.

    If your certificate shows "GB 123 456 789", enter 123456789.

    Submit rejects these forms:

    • GB123456789, which has a GB prefix.
    • 123 456 789, which has spaces.
    • 12345678, which has 8 digits.

    Bundles

    A bundle is what your account can file and how many tokens it gets. It holds no VAT registration number and no HMRC connection. You enter the number on the VAT form, and HMRC asks you to authorise each submission.

    To get a bundle, request a day pass or choose one on the Bundles page.

    • Resident VAT costs £9.99 a year or £0.99 a month and gives 100 tokens each month. You can submit VAT returns to HMRC and make pass links for other people.
    • Resident Ltd costs £9.99 a year or £0.99 a month and gives 100 tokens each month. File accounts and confirmation statements to Companies House.
    • Resident Pro costs £39.99 a year or £3.99 a month and gives 100 tokens each month. You can submit VAT returns to HMRC, file accounts with Companies House, and make pass links for other people.
    • Day pass is free and gives 3 tokens for 24 hours. You can submit VAT returns to HMRC.

    Each filing uses one token. Cancel any time. See the Bundles page to subscribe.

    1. Sign in and open the Bundles page.
    2. Click Manage Subscription on the bundle. This opens Stripe's billing portal.
    3. Cancel the subscription in the portal.

    Remove takes a bundle off your account. It does not cancel the subscription, and the Manage Subscription button is on the bundle's row. Cancel in the portal before you remove the bundle.

    No page deletes an account. Write to support@diyaccounting.co.uk from the email address of your account and ask for it to be deleted. Cancel any subscription first.

    We delete your bundles, sign-in data and audit logs within 30 days. The law makes us keep HMRC submission receipts for 7 years, and we anonymise them where we can. See the privacy policy for what is kept.

    Connection

    Submit sends you to HMRC's page in the same tab and brings you back after you authorise. If that fails:

    1. Refresh the page and try again. Your sign-in may have expired while the page was open.
    2. Check HMRC service availability for a fault.
    3. Try a different browser, or clear the cookies for this site.

    1. Sign up for Making Tax Digital for VAT at gov.uk/vat-record-keeping, if you have not already.
    2. Wait 24 to 48 hours. HMRC needs time to start your Making Tax Digital access.
    3. Sign in to DIY Accounting Submit with your Google or Microsoft account.
    4. Request a day pass or choose a bundle on the Bundles page.
    5. Check your obligations to see which VAT periods HMRC expects returns for.
    6. Enter your 9-digit VAT registration number and your figures on the VAT form.

    An HMRC authorisation lasts 18 months, or ends when you remove the software's authority.

    To authorise again:

    1. Start a submission. Submit sends you to HMRC.
    2. Enter your Government Gateway user ID and password on HMRC's page.
    3. Give DIY Accounting Submit permission to act for you.

    A business checks or removes the software's authority at tax.service.gov.uk/applications-manage-authority. An agent uses the agent services account.

    Production sends your filing to HMRC for real. Sandbox sends it to HMRC's test service, and nothing real is filed. Preview lets you try the screens, and nothing is sent.

    • Production needs your Government Gateway user ID and password. A submission is a legally binding VAT return.
    • Sandbox uses test credentials, and any VAT registration number works. A submission does not affect real tax records.
    • Preview shows the screens of a filing that is not yet available. Nothing goes to HMRC or Companies House.

    The availability table gives the status of each filing.

    If you lose access to the account you sign in with, nothing is lost at HMRC. Your HMRC authorisation belongs to your Government Gateway, not to your sign-in provider, and HMRC keeps its record of every VAT return you submitted.

    To carry on:

    1. Recover the account with its provider: Google's account recovery process for a Google account, or Microsoft's account recovery process for a Microsoft account.
    2. Or sign in with a new Google or Microsoft account.
    3. Request a day pass or choose a bundle on the Bundles page.
    4. Authorise with HMRC again the next time you submit.

    Receipts stay with the account you signed in with. If you cannot recover that account, write to support@diyaccounting.co.uk.

    Errors

    This error means the VAT registration number has the wrong form. It must be 9 digits with no spaces, letters or GB prefix: 123456789, not GB123456789 or 123 456 789.

    If your number is correct and Submit still rejects it:

    • Check that the business is registered for Making Tax Digital for VAT, not only for VAT.
    • Wait 24 to 48 hours after a new registration.

    HMRC rejected the submission because a return for this period already exists. This happens when you already submitted for the period, or when someone else, such as an accountant, submitted for the same VAT registration number. Check your receipts. In VAT obligations the period shows as Fulfilled with a received date.

    You are submitting for a period that HMRC does not expect a return for. Use Check VAT obligations to see which periods are open. Then:

    1. Match the VAT period to an Open obligation exactly.
    2. Check that you have not already submitted for this period. It would show as Fulfilled.
    3. Check that the VAT registration number is correct.

    Clearing browser data signs you out and removes the settings this site keeps in your browser. Your receipts stay in your account. Try these first:

    • Open a private or incognito window.
    • Try a different browser.
    • Clear the cookies for submit.diyaccounting.co.uk only.

    If the problem stays, send us a support request or email support@diyaccounting.co.uk.

    Receipts

    1. A receipt appears when the submission succeeds. It shows your Form Bundle Number. Save or print it.
    2. Click Receipts in the navigation bar to see all your submissions.
    3. Each receipt shows the date, the VAT registration number, the period and the HMRC reference.

    Receipts are stored in your account. You can open them from any device when you are signed in.

    If Submit showed a success message with a Form Bundle Number, HMRC received the return. A difference between the two usually has one of these causes:

    1. HMRC's systems can take a few hours to show a new return. Check again later.
    2. You may be checking a different period from the one you submitted.
    3. You may be checking a different VAT registration number. Check that you look at the same business in HMRC's portal.

    Your receipt shows the Form Bundle Number, for example 425379263134. Quote it to HMRC to prove the submission.
