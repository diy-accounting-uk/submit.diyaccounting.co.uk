1. What the Service does

DIY Accounting Submit is a web application that helps UK businesses and their accountants send filings to HMRC and Companies House, and keep the books behind them. The Service offers:

  • VAT (Making Tax Digital). View VAT obligations, liabilities, payments and penalties. Submit VAT returns to HMRC. View submitted returns and receipts.
  • Income Tax Self Assessment (Making Tax Digital for Income Tax). Manage business details and obligations, send quarterly updates for self-employment and UK property, make annual submissions and adjustments, work with losses and claims, view tax calculations and make the final declaration. This is available in HMRC's sandbox (test) environment until HMRC approves the Service for production.
  • Companies House. Search for a company. Change a registered office address or registered email address. File micro-entity accounts, small company accounts and confirmation statements. File a PSC verification statement. Changes of registered office and registered email address go to the live register. Accounts, confirmation statements and PSC verification statements are available in the Companies House sandbox (test) environment until Companies House approves the Service for live filing.
  • Company Tax Return (CT600). Prepare a company tax return for HMRC. This is a preview.
  • DIYA-GL books. Save and load DIYA-GL books in your account.
  • MCP server. Read and edit a book, and file from it, through an AI assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol.
  • Receipts and history. View receipts of earlier submissions.

Each feature shows its status (production, sandbox or preview) on its own page. A sandbox or preview feature sends nothing to the live service of HMRC or Companies House.

7. Fraud Prevention Headers

In compliance with HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) regulations, the Service collects and transmits fraud prevention headers with each VAT return submission. This is a legal requirement for all MTD-compliant software.

Data collected includes:

  • Public IP address
  • Device identifier (generated fresh for each submission, not stored persistently)
  • Browser user agent, timezone, screen resolution, and window size
  • User identifier (Cognito user ID)
  • Vendor information (application name and version)

This data helps HMRC identify and prevent fraudulent VAT submissions and supports criminal prosecutions for tax fraud. You consent to this collection when you authorise the Service through HMRC's OAuth flow. For full details, see the HMRC Fraud Prevention section of our Privacy Policy.

22. Affiliate and referral passes

These terms take effect when the first affiliate is approved.

A pass is a link that gives the person who follows it 7 days of free VAT submissions. The account that made the pass earns a commission when the person it brings in becomes a paying customer.

What you earn

  • The commission is 30% of the net amount the referred customer pays us, for their first 12 months.
  • A customer who shares a pass earns credit on their own subscription. We release the credit 30 days after the payment it comes from. Credit is applied monthly. Credit is capped at 12 free months in total.
  • An approved affiliate earns cash. We release cash 120 days after the payment it comes from. Cash is capped at £100 a month and £1,000 a tax year, and we pay it only when it reaches £20.
  • We hold a commission for review when a cap is passed, when one pass brings in more than 5 conversions in a day, or when a signal of fraud appears.

Refunds and cancellations

If a referred customer is refunded, or disputes the payment, we reverse the matching commission. If we have not paid it, it is not paid. If we have paid it, we take it from your next commission. We take back unused credit.
