3 to 160 characters, usually ending in LIMITED or LTD; a company registered in Wales may end it in CYFYNGEDIG or CYF instead. Companies House records names in capital letters.

This company is registered in Wales, so its name may end in CYFYNGEDIG or CYF in place of LIMITED or LTD.

Your name must usually end in either ‘Limited’ or ‘Ltd’. You can include the Welsh equivalents ‘Cyfyngedig’ and ‘Cyf’ instead if you registered the company in Wales.

Your name cannot be the same as another registered company’s name.

Source: GOV.UK, Set up a private limited company: choose a company name (checked 22 November 2026)

This files a change of name by a special resolution. A change by the articles of association is filed on paper, on form NM04.

A company can change its name either by: a special resolution; permission given in the company’s articles of association.

You can use the Companies House online service to file changes of name by special resolution only.

Source: GOV.UK, Make changes to your private limited company: Company name (checked 22 November 2026)

It costs £20 to file a change of name online, or £85 for the same-day service.

Source: GOV.UK, Change a company name (NM01) (checked 22 November 2026)

Companies House charges the same for a change of name filed by software. You pay that fee and the card charge here before the filing is sent: £24.69 for the standard service, or £104.12 for the same-day service. If Companies House rejects the filing, the payment is refunded. In this sandbox nothing is registered and no fee is charged.

Your name also cannot contain a ‘sensitive’ word or expression, or suggest a connection with government or local authorities, unless you get permission.

Source: GOV.UK, Set up a private limited company: Choose a name (checked 22 November 2026)
