The shares allotted

A company returns an allotment within one month of making it. Companies House charges no fee for this filing. Everything on it appears on the public record.

Allotment dates

If all shares were allotted on the same day, enter that date as the from date. If shares were allotted over a period of time, enter both dates.

Shares allotted

Give details of the shares allotted, including bonus shares. Amounts are for each share, in the currency above.

Share capital before the allotment

Including both the nominal value and any share premium. Enter 0 if the shares are fully paid.

Statement of capital after the allotment

The issued share capital this return states: the capital before, with the shares allotted added.
