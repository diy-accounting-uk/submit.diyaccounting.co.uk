Kind of person with significant control

An ‘other registrable person’ is: a corporation sole; a government or government department of a country or territory or a part of a country or territory; an international organisation whose members include two or more countries or territories (or their governments); a local authority or local government body in the UK or elsewhere.

Service address

This is the address that will appear on the public record. It does not have to be the individual's usual residential address.

The service address

If you give the individual's residential address here, it will appear on the public record.

Usual residential address

This address is not shown on the public record. It cannot be the service address when the service address is the company's registered office, and it cannot be a PO Box.

The usual residential address

The address where the individual usually lives.

Identity verification (optional)

Only complete this if the person has verified their identity and shared their personal code with you. A personal code is an 11 character code that is given to a person after they've verified their identity with Companies House.
