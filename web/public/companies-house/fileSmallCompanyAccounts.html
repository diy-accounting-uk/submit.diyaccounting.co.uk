What Companies House receives

Section 444 of the Companies Act 2006 lets a small company deliver accounts without its profit and loss account. The company's members still receive the full accounts.

Profit and loss format

Schedule 1 to the Small Companies and Groups (Accounts and Directors' Report) Regulations 2008 gives both formats. Use the format the company used last year.
