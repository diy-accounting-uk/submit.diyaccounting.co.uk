Which company?

8 characters, for example 00000001. Do not know it? Search Companies House.

Which period, and its new end date?

Company ().

You can shorten your company’s financial year as many times as you like - the minimum period you can shorten it by is 1 day.

You cannot change your company’s year end when its accounts are overdue.

Source: GOV.UK, Change your company’s year end (checked 22 November 2026)

You cannot extend a period beyond 18 months unless the company is in administration.

You may not extend periods more than once in five years unless you fall into one of the following categories.

  • The company is in administration.
  • You have specific approval from the Secretary of State (please enclose a copy).
  • You are extending the company's accounting reference period to align with that of a parent or subsidiary undertaking established in the United Kingdom.
  • You are submitting the form on behalf of an overseas company.
Source: Companies House, AA01 Change of accounting reference date (checked 22 November 2026)
