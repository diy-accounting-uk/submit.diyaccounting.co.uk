# DORA 2026 via vendor blogs

- No primary DORA 2026 report was in the results; the figures come from vendor blogs and conflict.
- DORA replaced the elite/high/medium/low tiers with seven team profiles and added rework rate as a fifth metric.
- Vendors still cite 16.2% of organisations deploying on demand and 9.4% with lead time under one hour; one blog gives 9.4% for deployment frequency instead. Unverified.
- LinearB 2026 elite: more than 1.2 releases per service, cycle time under 25 hours, change failure under 1%. A different measure from DORA's.
