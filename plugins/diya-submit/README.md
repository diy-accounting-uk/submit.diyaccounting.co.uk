# DIY Accounting Submit for Claude

Work out a UK VAT return under Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT from the bookkeeping you keep in DIY Accounting Submit, check HMRC's nine boxes and the lines behind them with Claude, and read the HMRC receipt afterwards.

It is for a VAT-registered UK business that keeps its bookkeeping in DIY Accounting Submit, for example a limited company (Ltd) or a sole trader. The plugin gives no tax advice.

## Use it

Connect the DIY Accounting Submit connector and sign in. Ask Claude to work out your VAT return for a quarter. Claude opens the DIYA-GL book you name, shows the nine boxes with the lines behind them, and gives you a link to the VAT form with the figures filled in. You open the link and file the return yourself on the Submit page, where you tick the declaration. Ask Claude to read the receipt when you are done.

## Data

The plugin sends the book you ask Claude to open, and the period you name, to submit.diyaccounting.co.uk through the connector. It stores nothing itself. No tool in the connector files a return or pays HMRC.
