# Competitor Analysis

UK market for MTD VAT filing and accounting spreadsheets, surveyed 2026-08-25.

## Our HMRC listing

DIY Accounting Submit is listed on HMRC's [Find software for Making Tax Digital for VAT](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/making-tax-digital-software) finder, one of about 466 recognised products. The listing reads:

> **DIY Accounting Submit** is suitable for businesses or agents.
> Software type: Record-keeping software, Bridging software.
> Features: Submit VAT returns, View submitted VAT returns, Check what VAT you owe, View VAT payment history.
> Accessibility: blindness or impaired vision, deafness or impaired hearing, motor or physical difficulties and cognitive impairments.
> Link: https://submit.diyaccounting.co.uk/

**Gap: our listing has no "There is a free version of this software" flag.** The neighbouring "DIY Tax" entry has one. Submit has a free tier, and "free version" is a common filter for our target customer, so people filtering that way never see us. Ask HMRC's web publishing team (Sam Evans / SDST, thread from May 2026) to add the flag.

We have no entry on the [MTD Income Tax software finder](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/find-making-tax-digital-income-tax-software/software-results). Submit is VAT-only today.

## Spreadsheet template competitors

- [ANNA's free MTD templates](https://anna.money/free-tools/mtd-xls-templates/): free Excel and Google Sheets VAT templates that funnel into ANNA's paid filing. The closest match to our spreadsheet-plus-filing pairing.
- [Mr.SpreadSheet](https://mrspreadsheet.co.uk/): accountant-authored Excel bookkeeping templates, low one-off prices, positioned as MTD-ready.
- [Business Accounting Basics](https://www.businessaccountingbasics.co.uk/excel-bookkeeping-templates/): 29 free templates, 395,000+ downloads.
- Etsy and Gumroad sellers: generic bookkeeping templates for a few pounds, not UK-tax-specific, not MTD-aware.

No template seller except ANNA pairs templates with a first-party HMRC-recognised filing path. That pairing is ours to press.

## MTD Income Tax timeline

Mandation phases by gross trading and property income ([ICAS](https://www.icas.com/news-insights-events/news/tax/further-updates-to-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-announced)):

- **6 April 2026**: over £50,000 (already live).
- **6 April 2027**: over £30,000.
- **6 April 2028**: over £20,000.

Once in, a taxpayer stays in. Quarterly updates plus a year-end declaration, all through software. HMRC accepts spreadsheets plus bridging software as valid digital record keeping for ITSA, provided every step from record to submission is a digital link ([AbraTax](https://www.abratax.co.uk/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/)).

The 2027 and 2028 waves (£20,000–£30,000 sole traders) are our historic spreadsheet customer. No first-party DIY Accounting product serves them yet.

## Positioning

**Free alone does not differentiate.** My Tax Digital and 100PcVatFreeBridge are already free with no catch. Free wins only against the £20–£90/yr bridges and the £7–£10/mo suites.

**Our two real edges:**

1. **Spreadsheet and filing under one brand.** Most bridges bolt a filing step onto someone else's spreadsheet. We supply the template and the filing path together. Only ANNA matches this. Tightening the pairing, so a DIY spreadsheet flows into Submit without re-keying, is hard for bridging-only vendors to copy.
2. **The ITSA wave.** Sole traders mandated in 2027 and 2028 are price-sensitive, Excel-comfortable, single-trade filers. The big suites will chase them at £7–£10/mo. My Tax Digital will chase them free. A product that is near-free and spreadsheet-native sits between those, and we can be listed on the ITSA finder before the wave lands.

Supporting asset: the business has traded since 2004, which outlasts nearly every bridging-only vendor. Worth stating plainly on the site.
